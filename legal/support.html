Common questions

A cheat doesn't work. What should I do?

Check that you've picked the right game and platform, since codes differ between them. Some games also block saving or achievements while a cheat is on. If a code is still wrong, email us the game, platform, and cheat name.

Why does the App need the internet?

It downloads the game content and keeps a copy on your device, so what you've already opened still loads without a connection. New content, news, and map downloads need a connection.

How do I turn notifications on or off?

Use the Notifications option in the App's ⋮ More options menu on the Home screen, or your device's settings.

How do I change my advertising or tracking choices?

Use Privacy options in the ⋮ More options menu on the Home screen, where it's available in your region. On iOS, you can also change tracking in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

How do I delete my data?

The App has no accounts. Deleting it removes what it stores on your device, including favourites, downloaded maps, and streak progress. For anything else, see Deleting your data.
