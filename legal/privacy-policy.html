Privacy Policy

Effective date: July 30, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how GTA Cheats Guide ("the App"), developed by Code Library ("we," "us," "our"), collects, uses, and discloses information when you use the App.

GTA Cheats Guide is an unofficial, fan-made reference application and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Rockstar Games, Take-Two Interactive, or any of their subsidiaries. All game names and trademarks referenced belong to their respective owners.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Information Collected Automatically

We use Firebase Crashlytics and Firebase Analytics (Google) to help us understand app stability and usage. This may include:

  • Device model, operating system version, and app version
  • Crash logs and diagnostic data
  • General usage data (e.g., screens viewed, features used)
  • Approximate location (country/region level only, derived from IP address)

We do not use this information to identify you personally.

1.2 Advertising Data

The App displays advertisements through Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK). To serve ads, Google and its advertising partners may collect:

  • Your advertising identifier (Android Advertising ID)
  • Device and app information
  • IP address and approximate location
  • Ad interaction data (impressions, clicks)

If you are located in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, or Switzerland, you will be shown a consent form before any ads are requested, allowing you to consent, decline, or manage your preferences in detail. You can change your choice at any time via Settings → Privacy Options within the App.

You can also opt out of personalized advertising generally through your device's ad settings (Android: Settings → Google → Ads → Opt out of Ads Personalization).

1.3 Purchase Information

The App offers a one-time, non-consumable "Remove Ads" in-app purchase, processed entirely through Google Play Billing. We do not collect or store your payment details (card numbers, billing address, etc.) — these are handled directly by Google Play. Our app only stores a local flag on your device indicating whether you have purchased ad removal, so that ads can be correctly disabled.

1.4 Information We Do Not Collect

  • We do not require account creation, sign-up, or login of any kind.
  • We do not collect your name, email address, or phone number.
  • We do not access your contacts, photos, or media, except when you explicitly choose to save a wallpaper image to your device gallery, which uses a standard OS permission prompt at that moment only.
  • We do not sell your personal information.

2. How We Use Information

  • To display and measure the performance of advertisements
  • To diagnose crashes and improve app stability and performance
  • To remember your ad-free (premium) purchase status across app sessions
  • To understand aggregate usage patterns and improve the App over time

3. Third-Party Services

The App relies on the following third-party services, each governed by its own privacy policy:

  • Google / Firebase (Firestore, Storage, Crashlytics, Analytics)
  • Google AdMob
  • Google Play Billing

We encourage you to review these third parties' privacy policies to understand how they process data.

4. Children's Privacy

GTA Cheats Guide is intended for a general audience and is not directed at children under the age of 13 (or the relevant age of digital consent in your region). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us using the details below so we can address it.

5. Data Retention & Security

Analytics and crash data are retained according to Google Firebase's standard retention periods. We do not maintain our own servers storing personal user data beyond the local, on-device storage described above (e.g., your ad-free purchase status).

6. Your Rights & Choices

  • You may manage or withdraw advertising consent at any time via Settings → Privacy Options in the App.
  • You may opt out of personalized advertising through your device's system settings.
  • You may request information about data associated with your device by contacting us below.
  • Residents of the EEA, UK, and certain US states may have additional rights under GDPR, UK GDPR, or applicable state privacy laws (e.g., access, deletion, or objection rights) — contact us to exercise these.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date. Continued use of the App after changes constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
